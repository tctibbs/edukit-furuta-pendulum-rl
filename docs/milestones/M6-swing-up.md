# Milestone 06: Swing-up

Swing from rest to upright and hold, with no manual lift. The policy performs the swing-up itself.

## Goals

- [ ] A policy swings up and holds from rest in simulation.
- [ ] Success rate is reported over a fixed seed set.
- [ ] The policy is compared against a scripted swing-up on the same starts.
- [ ] The policy swings up and holds on the physical unit, or the transfer gap is characterized with evidence.
- [ ] The policy is compared against the manual's [LQR baseline](../design-inputs/references/edukit-manual.md) on the same disturbances and the same hardware, in one session.
