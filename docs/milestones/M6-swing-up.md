# Milestone 06: swing-up

Swing from rest to upright and hold, with no manual lift. The policy performs the swing-up itself.

## Goals

- [ ] A policy swings up and holds from rest in simulation.
- [ ] The evaluation reports success rate over a fixed seed set.
- [ ] The evaluation compares the policy against a scripted swing-up on the same starts.
- [ ] The policy swings up and holds on the physical unit, or evidence characterizes the transfer gap.
- [ ] One session on the same hardware compares the policy against the manual's [LQR baseline](../design-inputs/references/edukit-manual.md), a Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR), under the same disturbances.
